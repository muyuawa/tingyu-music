## Universal album color engine

`Muyu.Core/AlbumColorEngine.cs` is pure math with no song names or track identifiers. `MainWindow.Appearance.cs` uses a 32×32 straight RGBA decode once per track. Every nontransparent pixel contributes its alpha-weighted perceived brightness and saturation. A 4-bit RGB histogram retains dark, white, neutral and muted bins. Population determines the dominant color; square-root population and chroma score choose the accent without losing small colored details. Mean saturation below 0.15 uses metal silver #C0C8D6. The five roles include dominant, vibrant, deep ambient (encoded perceived Y ≈0.12), light ambient (≈0.93), and foreground. These brightness units are distinct from WCAG relative luminance.

No artwork, malformed data or failed decoding uses #11151D / #F8F9FA and #245FCB. The latest user correction makes library and player share one scene. Mean brightness below 0.35 forces a dark scene on both pages; child theme scopes change without writing the saved global theme preference. Navigation, search, popovers and window controls follow the same effective foreground theme. Missing artwork follows that global theme. White artwork receives stronger soft shadow; dark artwork mixes 5% ambient color into the shadow.

Library and Now Playing share the same deep/light ambient base and five large radial GPU color fields with 30–45 second compositor drift. Page changes preserve field positions, colors, animation progress and background blur; no separate library wash or hero gradient is shown. The user's latest instruction supersedes the earlier 6%/15% wash and 280 DIP library hero proposal. Colors transition in 800ms when the combined old/new scene envelope safely supports text contrast. Theme changes and rare extreme transitions outside that bound switch immediately. Background blur is a shared persisted 0–80 DIP setting (0.5 steps); cover and lyrics remain sharp. Economy caps its effective value at 8 DIP.

Text contrast uses gamma-linearized sRGB and (Lmax+0.05)/(Lmin+0.05), following [W3C G18](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G18) and [Understanding contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html). The engine bounds each color channel across all field alpha combinations, conservatively considering encoded and linear RGB composition. A transition guard also bounds old/new canvas and field colors with the maximum old/new alpha, choosing readable transitional text/opacity or declining an unsafe color animation. It limits field intensity when needed, chooses readable primary/secondary/accent colors, and derives a minimum inactive lyric opacity. Randomized palette/field composite checks verify nominal colors ≥4.5:1. Blur is limited to 0.6 DIP. The 60/80 DIP lyric alpha edge masks intentionally disappear; antialiased glyph pixels, the fade region, photographic artwork and arbitrary content seen through glass are not claimed to pass a whole-screen WCAG audit. The scoped XAML foregrounds and rendered progress track are verified in actual UI captures for all five artwork classes in both themes and pages.
